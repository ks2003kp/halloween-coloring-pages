## Step-by-Step Coloring Tips

Explain practical techniques in structured paragraphs or ordered steps:

1. **Start with Lighter Tones**: Lay down your base highlights (such as sunny yellows or pale oranges) before adding deep purples or charcoal blacks.
2. **Blend from Edges Inward**: Darken the pumpkin ribs or ghost shadows toward the outer contour to create a 3D rounded appearance.
3. **Add Festive Accents**: Use white gel pens or metallic markers for twinkling stars and potion bubbles.
